# Product context

- **OpsFlow**: the fictional brand used for the public portfolio and mock CRM data.
- **Workflow prototype**: an interactive front-end demonstration of one business process. It is not a production CRM.
- **Pilot**: one workflow, up to four screens, desktop and mobile, mock data, hosted preview, source files, and two revision rounds.
- **Funded milestone**: escrow or an allowed deposit confirmed before work starts.
- **Representative**: the adult who legally owns the approved account, contracts with the client, completes verification, and receives payment where platform rules allow it.
- **Compliance gate**: the documented check of age, identity, country, tax, contracting, and withdrawal rules. Outreach stays blocked until every required row passes.
- **First paid US client**: a US customer who agrees to a defined scope and funds a milestone or pays a deposit through the approved route.

## Boundaries

The public demo is derived from reusable interaction patterns, not source-company data or branding. Claims describe behavior verified in this repository. Backend, integrations, migration, and production operation are excluded unless separately scoped after discovery.
